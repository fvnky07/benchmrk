Your fitness data, visualized like never before.

## Analytics Dashboard

### New Charts & Insights

- **Volume progression** - Track total training volume over time
- **Body composition trends** - Visualize weight and body fat changes
- **Strength curves** - See your strength progression across exercises
- **Training frequency heatmap** - Never miss a workout again
- **Muscle group balance** - Ensure proportional development

### Advanced Metrics

- Estimated 1RM calculations with Epley, Brzycki, and Lombardi formulas
- Training intensity distribution (light, moderate, heavy days)
- Recovery metrics and recommendations
- Predicted progress based on current trajectory

### Custom Reports

- Generate monthly performance reports
- Export data to CSV for external analysis
- Compare different time periods side-by-side
- Set custom date ranges for analysis

## Improvements

- Faster chart loading with lazy rendering
- Better mobile chart interactions
- Dark mode optimized charts
- Printable reports for trainers/coaches

## Pro Tip

Use the new "Compare Periods" feature to see how your current training block stacks up against previous ones!
