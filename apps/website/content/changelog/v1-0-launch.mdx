🎉 Benchmrk is officially live on iOS and Android!

## Welcome to Benchmrk

After months of development and beta testing with over 1,000 users, we're thrilled to launch benchmrk to the world. Our mission is to make fitness tracking smarter, faster, and more insightful through AI-powered coaching.

## Core Features

### Lightning-Fast Workout Logging

- Log exercises in under 3 seconds
- Smart exercise search with AI
- Auto-fill from previous workouts
- Swipe gestures for quick actions

### AI-Powered Coaching

- Real-time form feedback
- Progressive overload suggestions
- Deload week recommendations
- Exercise substitutions

### Comprehensive Analytics

- Track strength progression over time
- Volume and intensity metrics
- Body composition tracking
- Exercise performance history

### Beautiful Design

- Clean, intuitive interface
- Dark mode support
- Smooth animations
- Gesture-based navigation

## What Makes Us Different

Unlike other fitness apps, benchmrk focuses on **intelligent insights** rather than just data collection. Our AI analyzes your training patterns and provides actionable recommendations to help you reach your goals faster.

## Getting Started

1. Download the app from the App Store or Google Play
2. Create your account (takes 30 seconds)
3. Set your fitness goals
4. Start your first workout with AI guidance
