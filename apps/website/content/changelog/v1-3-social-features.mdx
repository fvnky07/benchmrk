Connect with friends and the benchmrk community!

## Social Features

### Workout Sharing

- Share your PRs directly to social media
- Create shareable workout summaries with beautiful graphics
- Tag friends in your workout posts

### Leaderboards

- Compete on exercise-specific leaderboards
- Weekly challenges with different focus areas
- Gym-based leaderboards for local competition
- Follow friends and see their progress

### Community Challenges

- Join monthly community challenges
- Track your ranking in real-time
- Earn badges for participation and achievements
- Connect with people training for similar goals

## Profile Enhancements

- Customizable profile pages
- Achievement showcase
- Training stats visualization
- Progress photo timeline

## Privacy Controls

We know privacy matters. All social features include granular privacy controls:

- Choose who can see your workouts (public, friends, private)
- Disable leaderboards if you prefer solo training
- Hide specific exercises or stats from your profile
