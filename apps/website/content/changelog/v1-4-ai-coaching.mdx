Introducing AI Coaching 2.0 with fully personalized workout recommendations.

## New Features

### Smart Workout Plans

Our AI now generates completely personalized workout plans based on:

- Your fitness goals (strength, hypertrophy, endurance)
- Available equipment
- Time constraints
- Recovery status
- Past performance data

### Adaptive Training

The AI automatically adjusts your workouts based on:

- Fatigue levels detected from previous sessions
- Rate of progression
- Injury prevention patterns
- Deload week recommendations

### Exercise Form Analysis

- **Real-time form feedback** using computer vision (beta)
- Rep tempo tracking
- Range of motion analysis
- Safety alerts for dangerous form

## Improvements

- Enhanced voice commands during workouts
- Better exercise recommendations when equipment is unavailable
- Improved rest time suggestions based on heart rate recovery
