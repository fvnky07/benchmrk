Track your nutrition alongside your workouts for complete fitness insights.

## Nutrition Features

### Macro & Calorie Tracking

- Log meals with extensive food database (500,000+ items)
- Barcode scanner for packaged foods
- Custom meal creation and saving
- Macro distribution visualization
- Daily calorie goals with smart recommendations

### AI Meal Recommendations

- Meal suggestions based on training intensity
- Pre/post-workout nutrition timing
- Macro targets adjusted for workout days vs rest days
- Recipe suggestions to hit your macros

### Integration with Workout Data

- See nutrition impact on performance
- Correlation between calorie intake and strength gains
- Energy availability tracking
- Hydration reminders based on workout intensity

## Food Logging Improvements

- Quick-add favorite meals
- Meal templates for recurring foods
- Restaurant menu integration (beta)
- Voice-activated food logging

## Smart Insights

The AI now analyzes the relationship between your nutrition and performance:

- "Your squat PR came after 3 days of hitting protein goals"
- "Consider increasing carbs on leg day for better performance"
- "You're most consistent when you meal prep on Sundays"
